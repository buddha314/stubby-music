## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The player can lead a stream to a tree
The player SHALL be able to dig so that a stream from a burbler can reach a scrub oak,
and a stream that reaches a tree SHALL water it.

#### Scenario: Watering the tree
- **WHEN** the player digs from the burbler's stream to a dormant scrub oak and the stream reaches it
- **THEN** the tree is watered and the "Water the tree" chore completes
