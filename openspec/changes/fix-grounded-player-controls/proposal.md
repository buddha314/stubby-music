## Why
The inspection camera flies downward when looking down and requires RMB for look. Players need familiar grounded first-person controls.

## What Changes
- Start standing on the mudflat with a level view.
- Grounded WASD, free mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch.
- Capture the cursor; Escape or focus loss releases it; left click recaptures.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `player-controls`: grounded first-person movement and cursor ownership.

## Impact
Shared Bevy controller used by standalone and Jackdaw Play; no physics dependency.
