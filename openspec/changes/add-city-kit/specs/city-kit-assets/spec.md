## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Modular grid contract
City kit pieces SHALL share a 4 m structural bay and a 4 m floor-to-floor height in metres. Linear pieces SHALL have their origin at ground level on the left front snap corner, run along +X, and face Blender -Y (glTF +Z). Standalone pieces SHALL have their origin at the centre of their base. Pieces SHALL be built with identity transforms and no negative scale.

#### Scenario: Facades join and stack
- **WHEN** two facade bays of either variant are placed 4 m apart along X, and another is placed 4 m above
- **THEN** their walls and floor bands meet without gaps or coplanar overlaps

#### Scenario: Corners wrap the cut ends
- **WHEN** a corner pier or end cap is placed at a footprint corner or a run's free end
- **THEN** it encloses the walls' cut ends, so no wall end face is left visible

### Requirement: Declared acoustic classes
Every exported City material SHALL declare a Steam Audio preset from `stubby_core::AcousticOccluder::preset` (or "none") as glTF material extras `acoustic`. Large near-level meshes SHALL carry the same preset as node extras. Living and small parts SHALL stay out of the acoustic scene.

#### Scenario: Validation finds every class
- **WHEN** `tools/blender/check_city_kit.py` runs over the exported models
- **THEN** every material has a known acoustic class and no node names an unknown preset

### Requirement: Living light and signal colour
Luminous flora SHALL use a separate opaque core and translucent halo shell (alpha 0.2 to 0.4), authored at peak, in the bloomed accents. Machine signal lights SHALL be amber only. No piece SHALL animate on its own timer.

#### Scenario: Machines stay amber
- **WHEN** a machine piece (pedestal, pump house, water gate, lift) is inspected
- **THEN** its only signal light is amber, and it has interaction and audio anchors

### Requirement: Sockets instead of baked content
Plants, displays, exhibits, performers, recordings, notes and motes SHALL be sockets (childless empties with an `accepts` kind) on the piece's root, never geometry baked into structure.

#### Scenario: The gallery shows nothing until the game fills it
- **WHEN** the gallery display wall and hall stage are loaded
- **THEN** they carry `Display_0` and `Recording_source` anchors and no player content

### Requirement: Reproducible pieces and district
The kit SHALL be regenerated by headless Blender scripts into the repository's asset family layout, with editable sources marked as Blender assets. The reference district SHALL be built from linked instances of those sources, and its day, dusk and night presets SHALL share the same geometry.

#### Scenario: Regenerating the kit
- **WHEN** `create_city_kit.py` and `create_city_district.py` run
- **THEN** every piece's source, glb, prefab and profile, the manifest, the collider proxies and the district file are rewritten, and the layout and LOD policy checks pass
