## Why

Module assets (the VCOs and the clock) are the player-facing face of the synth. They
must be bound to the real `stubby_audio` modules behind them: placing one adds the
engine module, its parameters drive the engine, and its lights, display and knobs
follow the engine, so what the player sees keeps time with what they hear. This was
built in PR #17 (`crates/stubby_game/src/synth_modules.rs`) without a spec. This change
records what it promises and the work still open.

## What Changes

- Records the binding already on `main`: module profiles, the placed-module components
  (`SynthModule`, `ModuleParams`, `ModuleLights`), engine add/parameter/remove, lights
  driven by engine ticks through the shared pulse bus, the BPM display, knob rotation,
  per-instance materials, and the `--module-demo` showcase.
- Adds open work: a shared engine `ModuleId` allocator to replace the hand-picked
  ranges in `AGENTS.md`; VCO LEDs following real output level once the rack publishes
  meters; saw and triangle sound once the engine has those oscillators; and an
  on-screen check that the clock's flash lands within 10 ms of its tick.

## Capabilities
