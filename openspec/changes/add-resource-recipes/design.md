## Decisions
A square-wave item represents a recovered waveform resource/pattern, not an immediately audible oscillator. Recipes use stable string item IDs and integer quantities. The catalog validates unique IDs, existing item references and positive quantities. Crafting first aggregates and validates all inputs, checks output capacity, then commits the inventory update atomically; a failure never consumes ingredients. Repeated lines for the same item are aggregated.

Use a small Bevy-independent `stubby_recipes` crate with serde-backed data definitions. Item and resource data live in assets/recipes. The game holds an inventory resource and reports nearby harvestable bugs through a compact interaction prompt. E collects a lightning bug once and adds the catalog-configured square_wave quantity (initially two). Successful collection removes that bug’s model, ground pulse, light, and audio voice. Other bugs keep their independent clocks; inventory itself does not create an audio patch. Waveforms are the raw building blocks, analogous to mining coal. The bug’s recipe-book entry is Square wave, with VCO, LFO and gate roles. Other waveforms come from other assets. Do not turn this raw resource into a preselected finished module recipe.

The recipe book tracks discovered ingredient IDs separately from current inventory, so consuming a square-wave quantity later cannot erase knowledge of the waveform. O opens the in-game book; E mines the nearby bug’s pattern once.
