## Context

The game's theme is that music brings life to a bleak desert, and streams carry it. Open design work overlaps with this reference: `add-digging` has no mechanic yet, `add-fluid-quality-and-viscosity` has no rates or cleaning recipes, and streams cannot yet be shared or scheduled. The reference is a single secondary source, read from captions.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Keep the reference and what we took from it findable.
- Separate what the video says from what we made of it.
- Make the decisions needed before any idea is built explicit.

**Non-Goals:**

- Choosing a mechanic. That stays with the designer.
- Accurate depiction of Persian history or Iran, which would need its own research.
- Recreating any existing game.

## Decisions

**A research note plus a tracking change.** The content belongs in `docs/research/`, like our other references, and the change tracks decisions. Writing requirements for each idea now would imply commitments nobody has made.

**Summarize, do not store the transcript.** A timed summary is enough to find the moment in the video, and it avoids republishing someone's script.

**One idea, one change.** An adopted idea gets its own proposal, so this change never grows into a design for everything.

**Suggested first look, for the designer to overrule.** Dividing water by time on the rack: it turns water management into patching, needs no new terrain, and keeps every machine an instrument. It also gives streams something to do once there is more than one destination.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **One secondary source:** figures and history may be simplified. Check before relying on them.
- **Scope creep:** the ideas are attractive and broad. They stay out of the opening scene's milestone unless chosen.
- **Conflict with existing rules:** a finite water table contradicts infinite mines. The note lists this and similar tensions, to be decided, not worked around.
- **Cultural flattening:** borrowing from a real, living culture calls for care in naming and framing.
