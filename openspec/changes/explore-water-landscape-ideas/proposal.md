## Why

Damilee's video on Persian qanats and gardens shows a whole way of managing water with the environment: land, gravity, wind, shade, night and shared rules. It answers open questions in our water design, and its ideas fit the game's theme closely. This records it so it can be used later without watching it again. Nothing is committed yet.

## What Changes

- Add `docs/research/persian-water-systems.md`: the source, a timed summary of what the video describes, candidate game ideas, and the tensions they raise.
- Add a short process for turning an idea into its own change, or dropping it.
- Point `add-digging` and `add-fluid-quality-and-viscosity` at the note.
- **No code, assets or rule changes.** Ideas stay ideas until the designer picks them.
