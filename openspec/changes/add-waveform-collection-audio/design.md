## Approach
Use a shared waveform sound preset and graph composition for world pulses and collection cues. The square oscillator runs at 2200Hz with 15ms attack and 285ms exponential decay to -60dB, matching the new 300ms visual duration. Existing seeded clocks and intervals stay intact. Harvest emits its successful yield stacks; a generic waveform collection listener resolves catalog waveform metadata and plays one cue per distinct waveform type, irrespective of quantity. Each cue gets independent module IDs and a constant-high trigger, with cleanup after the sample-driven envelope finishes. Cleanup timers only remove silent nodes; they do not generate musical timing. Confirmation gain has no distance falloff. World audio remains spatially attenuated. Initial catalog quantity is 2, inspired by normal Satisfactory hand-mining, not a claim that bugs are infinite nodes. Square wave metadata and presets are provisional while that asset is developed.
