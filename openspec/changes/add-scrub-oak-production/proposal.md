## Why
Scrub oak trees are infinite lightning-bug mines. Improving their environment should increase production without making individual bugs inexhaustible.

## What Changes
- FUTURE implementation: scrub-oak trees produce finite lightning bugs repeatedly.
- Better water quality and light quality increase bug production.
- Mine capacity is infinite; individual emitted bugs remain exhaustible.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `scrub-oak-production`: environmentally improved renewable bug sources.

## Impact
Future mine production, environment quality and tree assets. Requirements only for now.
