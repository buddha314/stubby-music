## Why
Free mouse look needs a centered aiming reference. The player requested a sunflower outline.

## What Changes
- Add a small ivory sunflower outline with an open center and dark edging.
- Keep it centered across window sizes in standalone and Jackdaw Play.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `player-hud`: sunflower aiming reticle.

## Impact
Shared HUD and small transparent runtime texture, reproducible source generator.
