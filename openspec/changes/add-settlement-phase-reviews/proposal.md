## Why

Camp, Grove, Village and City give the mudflat-to-city arc four review stops.
A repeatable route lets us examine what each phase adds to the player's work
and how the same place looks after the world responds.

## What changes

- Define four provisional progression phases. Give each a player capability and
  a visible consequence that designers can review independently.
- Create four Jackdaw scenes at one site with a shared burbler, channel route,
  campfire and landmark. Use existing master prefabs and scene brushes for
  settlement forms still in design.
- Index the asset families by first use and record the remaining art and
  gameplay decisions. Keep phase entry rules open until the samples are reviewed.

## Capabilities

- New capability `settlement-phase-reviews` for comparing the same site at four
  stages of development.

## Impact

Adds review scenes, asset-planning notes and a bounded review selector.
Production progression remains an open design question.
