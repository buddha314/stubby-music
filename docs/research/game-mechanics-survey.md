# Mechanics from other games

A survey made 2026-11-20 for the roadmap (`docs/design/roadmap.md`): mechanics from other
games that suit **music and mythology restoring the world, and a society of art and
science growing from one human to a civilisation** (Satisfactory meets Farthest Frontier
meets VCV Rack). Each entry gives the mechanic, then how it could apply. These are ideas
for proposals, not decisions. Numbers in other games change between versions; take the
patterns, not the figures. Sources marked *(unverified)* were not opened during the survey.

## A. Progression and unlocks

- **Satisfactory, milestones and Space Elevator phases.** Milestones unlock buildings by
  tier; Elevator phases ask for bulk deliveries of project parts and open the next tiers;
  later phases reuse earlier parts. *Apply:* each settlement phase asks for a *sustained*
  output that reuses earlier ones (a stream holding clear for a day; three villages
  keeping a shared tempo), so early chains never go obsolete.
  [Project Assembly](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Project_Assembly) ·
  [Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones)
- **Satisfactory, MAM research and hard drives.** A side tree fed by found things; hard
  drives offer a choice of one of three alternate recipes. *Apply:* a listening post
  researches field recordings; rare finds offer one of three *alternate patches* for a
  machine (a campfire voiced through a band-pass). Still a visible patch, so a rack plays
  only what it shows. [Progression](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Progression)
- **Factorio, science packs.** Research consumes produced packs, each proving one more
  production layer. *Apply:* recordings of rising complexity as the research currency
  (a gated square; a sequence; a filtered sequence; two villages in sync). Cap at about
  five: Factorio grew from four to seven. [Science pack](https://wiki.factorio.com/Science_pack)
- **Factorio, blueprints.** *Apply:* patch sheets that copy a rack's cables and knobs to
  another machine of its kind, paid in cables; a mid-game unlock.
- **Factorio trains, Dyson Sphere Program.** A logistics tier that changes how distance
  feels; a visible megaproject. *Apply:* light rail as City logistics, not a goal flag; a
  civic work that keeps growing after its goal is met.
  [DSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyson_Sphere_Program) *(unverified)*
- **Spore, civilisation stages.** Five stages, earlier choices shaping later ones; widely
  criticised as separate shallow minigames. *Apply:* keep one set of verbs and widen scope;
  carry a temperament forward (the waveforms a village was founded on become its sound).
  [Stage](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Stage)

## B. Settlement growth and needs

- **Farthest Frontier, desirability and tiers.** Housing upgrades need desirability from
  decor and services, lowered by nuisances; town tiers need upgraded houses; immigrants
  avoid towns that cannot sustain them. *Apply:* Dancers arrive on surplus; desirability
  becomes *resonance* (sounding streams, clean air, flowering trees), and a droning,
  unpatched machine is a nuisance. Drop raiders, disease and starvation.
  [Guide](https://www.farthestfrontier.com/guide/) ·
  [Desirability](https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/farthest-frontier-desirability-guide)
- **Anno 1800, population tiers.** Basic needs gate a house's advance; luxury needs only
  raise happiness and income. *Apply:* water, food and shelter gate a Dancer tier; cultural
  needs (a nightly song, a festival) lift mood but never block; new tiers bring needs that
  pull the player into new chains. [Population](https://anno1800.fandom.com/wiki/Population) ·
  [Residential tiers](https://www.anno-union.com/en/devblog-residential-tiers/)
- **Against the Storm, needs, resolve and the hearth.** Species need different things;
  villagers leave when resolve falls; the hearth is the centre of every settlement.
  *Apply:* the campfire is the hearth and its reach sets where Dancers live; lineages could
  prefer different tempos; leaving is the only consequence.
  [Resolve](https://wiki.hoodedhorse.com/Against_the_Storm/Resolve) ·
  [Needs](https://wiki.hoodedhorse.com/Against_the_Storm/Needs)
- **Frostpunk, laws.** Dilemmas between two meters. *Apply:* charters, optional customs
  that trade one style for another (a rest day each seventh day, a children's choir); one
  positive meter, no moral blackmail.
  [Frostpunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostpunk) *(unverified)*
- **Banished, professions by count.** *Apply:* set how many Dancers tend, carry and build;
  never steer individuals.
  [Banished](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banished_(video_game)) *(unverified)*

## C. Logistics and trade

- **Anno 1800, trade routes between islands.** Islands produce different goods; higher
  tiers need goods from others. *Apply:* this is the Town condition: villages specialise by
  biome and trade along routes set once, ideally streams, so trade is audible.
  [Anno 1800](https://anno1800.fandom.com/wiki/Anno_1800)
- **Timberborn, districts.** Semi-independent areas with their own centres, linked by
  distribution posts. *Apply:* each village has its own clock; trading needs the two to
  agree on tempo (a tempo bridge, or locking clocks; both are whole multiples of the master
  clock already). [Weather](https://timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Weather)
- **Satisfactory, logistics tiers.** *Apply:* cleaner water flows faster (viscosity,
  already planned), so cleaning *is* the logistics upgrade; light rail is the long haul.
- **Farthest Frontier, trading post.** *Apply:* travelling troupes trade goods for
  performances; optional flavour.

## D. Ecology and restoration

- **Terra Nil.** Restore a region in phases, then recycle every building and leave only
  nature; players disliked removal breaking the rivers they had built. *Apply:* once a
  region runs clear, dismantle Camp-era dams and rough streams for a refund and let
  self-sustaining springs replace them, never breaking what the player relies on.
  [Terra Nil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terra_Nil) ·
  [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/terra-nil-asks-you-to-be-a-city-builder-and-then-remove-yourself-entirely)
- **Timberborn, droughts and badtides.** Forecast seasons when sources stop or turn
  contaminated; reservoirs and dams carry the town through. *Apply:* forecast *silent
  seasons* when burblers slow or the wind returns; still pools keep the music going; crops
  pause rather than die. [Drought](https://timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Drought) ·
  [Badtide](https://timberborn.org/wiki/weather-cycles/badtide)
- **Farthest Frontier, soil fertility and rotation.** *Apply:* the low-pass nutrient
  channel keeps fields fertile, giving the low-pass filters a purpose; rotation could be
  rotating the melody a field is played.
- **Eco, ecology, laws and specialisation.** Simulated species, voted laws, forced
  specialisation, research data as evidence for laws. *Apply:* villages cannot do
  everything, so they trade; recorder data (the air's history) justifies charters, science
  and art joined civically. [Eco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eco_(2018_video_game)) ·
  [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/eco-we-destroyed-the-meteor-heres-what-i-learned/)
- **Oxygen Not Included, overlays.** A cell-level fluid simulation made readable by
  overlays. *Apply:* keep our four water levels; the scanner gains overlays for air,
  clarity and tempo. [ONI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_Not_Included) *(unverified)*

## E. Music and art as play, and civic culture

- **Proteus.** Plants and creatures each have a musical signature; the soundtrack is what
  is near, changing with season and hour. *Apply:* each grown plant and returning creature
  adds a voice to the local mix, so a restored valley sounds restored and the ear leads.
  [Proteus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proteus_(video_game)) ·
  [Case study](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/the-sound-and-music-of-proteus-an-academic-case-study)
- **Rez, Tetris Effect, Hi-Fi Rush.** The world snaps to the beat. *Apply:* Dancers' work,
  harvest pops and construction ticks land on the local beat; a small bonus for player
  actions on the beat, never a penalty. Avoid Crypt of the NecroDancer's forced rhythm.
  [Hi-Fi Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hi-Fi_Rush) *(unverified)*
- **Civilization VI, great works and theming.** Great Musicians make works that draw
  tourism; museums double yields when works are themed. *Apply:* the gallery and concert
  hall show the player's own patches, recordings and scanner captures; a programme mixing
  three villages earns more; visiting Dancers are the tourism.
  [Great Work](https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Work_(Civ6))
- **Humankind, cultures per era.** *Apply:* each phase takes up a creation myth from the
  mythology research as its theme, each adding a trait that stacks into the civilisation's
  identity. [Humankind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humankind_(video_game)) *(unverified)*
- **Sky: Children of the Light, Cocoon.** *Apply:* festivals where Dancers gather at the
  amphitheatre and perform the player's patches; pure reward.
- **Black & White.** *Apply:* lightly: Dancers' devotion widens the player's hearing reach.

## F. Knowledge and discovery

- **Subnautica, scanning fragments.** Scanning enough fragments unlocks a blueprint.
  *Apply:* the field recorder is the scanner: recording a kind of source a few times learns
  it, and learned entries stay learned (as the recipe book already does).
  [Fragments](https://gamertweak.com/scanner-room-fragment-subnautica/)
- **Outer Wilds, knowledge as progression.** *Apply:* some unlocks are understanding:
  hearing the grotto raise pitch notes that rock openings are high-pass filters; myth
  fragments fill a log that points to unfound sources, alongside the lore guide and being
  atlas. [Outer Wilds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Wilds) *(unverified)*
- **Dorfromantik, small quests.** *Apply:* light requests from Dancers or nature ("the oak
  wants a slower song") as optional short goals in the hub.
  [Dorfromantik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorfromantik) *(unverified)*

## G. Pitfalls

- **Grim survival loops** (Frostpunk's starvation, Banished's spirals, Farthest Frontier's
  raids): use drift instead; Dancers leave, growth pauses, haze returns, all recoverable.
- **Micromanaging individuals:** professions by count, patch sheets, routes that run
  themselves.
- **Tech-tree bloat:** about five research currencies; alternates over new tiers.
- **Disconnected stages** (Spore): one set of verbs.
- **Dead early industry:** phases reuse old outputs; Terra Nil-style reclaiming.
- **Rhythm skill gates:** reward timing lightly, never require it.
- **Opaque simulation** (ONI): overlays, the scanner, discrete states.
- **Unforecast punishment:** telegraph silent seasons on the calendar.

## Ranked recommendations

1. Phases as sustained-output deliveries that reuse earlier outputs (Satisfactory).
2. Dancer tiers: basic needs gate, cultural needs lift mood (Anno 1800).
3. The recorder as a scanner, plus knowledge entries (Subnautica, Outer Wilds).
4. A clock per village; trade needs tempo agreement (Timberborn, Anno).
5. Restoration you can hear: each living thing adds a voice (Proteus).
6. Reclaiming early industry once a region is clear (Terra Nil).
7. Resonance as desirability, with no raids or deaths (Farthest Frontier).
8. A gallery and concert hall showing the player's own work, themed (Civilization VI).
9. Hearth-centred settlements; unhappy Dancers leave, nobody dies (Against the Storm).
10. Patch sheets and alternate patches (Factorio, Satisfactory).
