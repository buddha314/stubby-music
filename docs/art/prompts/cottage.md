# Cottage prompt

The first Dancer dwelling (`add-settlement-economy`: a cottage houses at most four).
Brief: `docs/art/cottage.md` · Images: `docs/art/cottage/`.

## Prompt

> Concept art design sheet for a stylised 3D game asset: a small off-grid homesteader
> cottage for a family of four, in a solarpunk world rebuilt from a desert mudflat. One
> storey with a sleeping loft under a steep roof; footprint about 6 × 8 m, ridge about
> 5.5 m, door 2 m tall for scale.
>
> Built by hand from scrap and grown materials: walls of rammed earth and
> mycelium-composite blocks in warm sand tones, framed with salvaged weathered timber and
> patched with rusted corrugated iron; the roof frame is naturally curving grown-wood ribs
> that flow down into the corner posts, as if grown in place rather than carpentered; a
> living sod roof with moss and short grasses, one panel of rusted corrugated sheet.
>
> High-tech off-grid systems lashed onto the crude structure, joins visible: a crisp row
> of near-black graphene solar panels on pale satin-titanium rails, strapped to rough
> timber posts with rusted iron bands; a slim vertical-axis wind turbine on a salvaged
> mast; rain gutters feeding a translucent water tank with a small filter column; a
> compact battery cabinet beside the door; a stovepipe chimney; a deep porch shaded by
> woven reed panels. Quiet musical touches: wind chimes made of scrap tubes under the
> eaves.
>
> Light is restrained and organic: a few soft bioluminescent fungi glowing faint cyan at
> the wall base, and one warm amber window glow from a hearth inside. No LED strips, no
> hard light points.
>
> Stylised and readable, Satisfactory meets Inside, softened toward solarpunk. Clean bold
> shapes, strong silhouette, simple PBR materials with flat base colours, moderate detail
> suitable for a low-to-mid-poly game model.
>
> Palette: sand #8c7656, rust iron #5e3a22, weathered wood #5a4a3a, titanium #b8bcc2,
> graphene #1a1c1f, moss #3f5a24, leaf #4f6a2a, small accents of cyan #33ffd9 and amber
> #ffb350.
>
> Layout: a large three-quarter hero view at eye height, with orthographic front, side and
> back elevations and a top view in a row beneath, all at the same scale. Neutral, even,
> overcast lighting on a plain light-grey background; the cottage on a simple patch of
> cracked dry mud ground.

## Negative prompt

> people, animals, text, labels, watermark, logo, photorealistic clutter, tiny noisy
> detail, LED strips, neon signs, glossy sci-fi chrome, dramatic lighting, heavy fog,
> perspective distortion in the elevations, inconsistent views between panels, modern
> suburban house, log-cabin cliché, snow, lush forest background
