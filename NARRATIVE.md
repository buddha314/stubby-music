# Stubby Music

Stubby Music is a game about the re-awakening of civilization through music. Our players begin in a barren, dessicated mudflat with noone around and few resources. They soon learn to use water and rhytmn to build a new society.

Scattered around the map are multiple mythic dieties and creatures, each associated with music and each in a biome similar to their mythic origins. The initial desert draws on native myths from the Mojave and US Southwest region.  Deities can be found drawn or carved into the rocks and trees, to be rejuvenated with song.

As the powers recover, small villages, then towns, then whole cities begin to emerge as a solarpunk utopia.
